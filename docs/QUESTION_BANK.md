# Question bank coverage

686 questions: 36 in Fundamentals and 26 in each of the other 25 content sections. This release adds 166 original depth items, revises the 20 original Fundamentals items to version 2, and revises s02-007 to version 2 with comparable motivation choices. All 520 prior IDs remain; saved attempts and retakes retain their stored versions. The bank contains 187 applied and 499 foundational items, with 679 single-answer and seven multiple-answer questions. Multiple-answer questions specify the required count and use exact-set grading.

Each question retains course-page citations. New depth items include integrated explanations addressing alternatives, subtopic metadata, and either objective-topic mapping or an explicit course-supplement designation. Authoring records report local source-text checks; this completion pass reviewed representative applied questions, all seven multiple-answer items, structural checks across the whole bank, and question version changes. It did not independently review every answer. Editorial difficulty is not calibrated to the live exam. Interactive PBQs and exhaustive objective-subtopic coverage remain future work.

Section practice includes all eligible questions up to 90. Capped section sets and cumulative practice first represent selected/studied sections, prioritize applied questions, and prefer less-repeated topics. A single Fundamentals exam now has 36 questions; another individual section has 26. Cumulative exams remain capped at 40.

Full forms A/B/C draw only from the 187 applied items. Each has 90 unique questions with domain quotas 11/20/16/25/18 and at least one item for each of the 28 primary objectives. Seeded selection remains reproducible for a particular bank; forms can overlap. Primary-objective representation is not complete leaf-topic coverage. Newly started forms may differ after bank updates; use Retake for the exact saved questions and versions.

Research, extracted guide text, authoring scripts/inputs, topic coverage reports, and the exam-style benchmark remain local-only and ignored. The public app/questions.json is self-contained. Local depth authoring uses research/build_depth.py, research/depth-items.psv, research/revise_foundations.py and research/build_objective_coverage.py. Do not rerun old expansion helpers blindly: they predate the revised bank and may overwrite later work.
