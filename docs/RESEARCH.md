## Research implications

The 26 course sections are not the 28 numbered SY0-701 objectives. Keep separate mappings and audit coverage at the topic level. The supplied material is a strong base: 405 guide pages, 21 objectives pages and 13 study-plan pages.

Newer references must not silently rewrite exam preparation. NIST incident-response Rev. 3 supersedes Rev. 2; authentication, sanitization, ATT&CK and OWASP also require version-specific citations. Record both course wording and any corroborated correction.

Local extraction revealed a likely typo on guide page 291: Group Policy Objective (GPO). Verify against an authoritative Microsoft reference before authoring that item; do not teach a known suspect expansion solely to match the notes.

No purchased exam banks, leaked questions or exam dumps were collected. The goal is original, evidence-backed practice with realistic application scenarios.

Before adding external excerpts to a public repository, check the particular license. Prefer citation links and original explanations. Source discovery does not grant redistribution rights.
